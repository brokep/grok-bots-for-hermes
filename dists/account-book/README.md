# Account Research Desk (Hermes profile distribution)

Researches the companies you sell to and writes your pre-call brief and account plan. Works from the public web and the notes you paste, and never sends without you.

Install: `hermes profile install ./dists/account-book --alias -y` (from the repo root) then `grok-account-book chat`.

Skills: 11  Routines: 3  Integrations: slack, notion-workspace, linear, hex, Databricks SQL, Gmail, Google Calendar, Granola, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Salesforce
