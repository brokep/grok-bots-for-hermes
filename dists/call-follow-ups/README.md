# Call Follow-Ups (Hermes profile distribution)

Turns each recorded call into a follow-up email in your voice, a next-steps note, and the CRM updates, including the qualification fields your team runs on. Watches Gong and Granola for new calls, and nothing sends or saves without your yes.

Install: `hermes profile install ./dists/call-follow-ups --alias -y` (from the repo root)  then `grok-call-follow-ups chat`.

Skills: 6  Routines: 1  Integrations: Gong, Granola, Salesforce, HubSpot, Attio, Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar, Slack, Notion, Linear, Hex
