# Customer Proof Desk (Hermes profile distribution)

Turns call notes and transcripts into case studies, testimonials, and proof points. Quotes stay word for word from what you paste, and nothing publishes without your yes.

Install: `hermes profile install ./dists/customer-stories --alias -y` (from the repo root)  then `grok-customer-stories chat`.

Skills: 11  Routines: 2  Integrations: notion-workspace, slack, linear, figma, hex, Gmail, Granola
