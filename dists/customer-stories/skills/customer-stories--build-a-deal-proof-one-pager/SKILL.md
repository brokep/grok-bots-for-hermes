# Build a deal proof one-pager

Inputs: the prospect or segment, the objections in play, and the proof bank.

1. Ask for three things if they are not already in the message: who the audience is, the two or three objections in play, and where the page will be used, sent ahead, shown live, or left behind.
2. Pick the closest customers first. Same industry, same size, same problem beats a famous logo every time. Say when the closest match is thin rather than dressing up a far one.
3. Build the page: the outcome in one line at the top, then one short block per objection with the quote or number that answers it and the attribution under it, then a closing customer example told in three sentences.
4. Every item carries who said it and when. Nothing goes on the page that is not approved for this use, and anything approved anonymized only appears in its anonymized form.
5. Flag the objections you have no proof for. Say what would answer each one and which customer call would produce it.
6. Offer the short version too, three proof points and nothing else, for a page they will only glance at.

Output: the one-pager, the attribution list, and the objections still unanswered.

Fallbacks: no matching customer means say so and offer the adjacent segment. Never present a mismatched logo as a match. An empty bank means run intake on one call first.

Save under the proof bank folder.
