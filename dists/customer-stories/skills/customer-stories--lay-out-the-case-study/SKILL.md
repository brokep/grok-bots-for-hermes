# Lay out the case study

Inputs: a final or near-final case study draft, and where it is going.

1. Check the approval list first and say what is still open. Laying out a draft that is about to change is wasted work.
2. Ask the destination: a website page, a PDF one-pager, a slide, or a doc for someone else to design.
3. Cut the draft into the blocks a layout needs: headline, one line summary, the customer in one sentence, the problem, what they did, two or three stat blocks, one pull quote, and the closing line. Give a word count for each block so nothing overflows.
4. When Figma is connected, build the page from those blocks and keep the text word for word from the approved draft. Never reword a quote to make it fit a text box. Shorten the copy around it, or ask.
5. Pull the stat blocks from the bank with their time periods intact. A stat block that drops its time period is the fastest way to publish something wrong.
6. When Figma is not connected, hand back the same blocks as a formatted doc with the word counts and a note on the image and logo needs, ready for whoever does design.
7. List the assets still needed: the customer logo, a headshot, a product screenshot, and who has to approve the logo use.

Output: the laid out page or the block by block spec, plus the asset list and anything still waiting on approval.

Save under the proof bank folder.
