# Ad Spend Watch (Hermes profile distribution)

Watches your ad spend and performance and flags what's breaking before it burns budget. Works from a pasted export, and never pauses a campaign without your yes.

Install: `hermes profile install ./dists/fuse --alias -y` (from the repo root) then `grok-fuse chat`.

Skills: 9  Routines: 3  Integrations: slack, notion-workspace, linear, hex, datadog, sentry, figma, Gmail, Google Sheets
