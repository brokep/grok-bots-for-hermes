# Recruiting Coordinator (Hermes profile distribution)

Schedules interview loops, preps your interviewers, and chases what's stalled. Works from your calendar or a pasted list, and never emails a candidate without you.

Install: `hermes profile install ./dists/mr-toms --alias -y` (from the repo root) then `grok-mr-toms chat`.

Skills: 8  Routines: 7  Integrations: slack, notion-workspace, linear, figma, Gmail, Google Calendar, Granola, Google Sheets, Ashby
