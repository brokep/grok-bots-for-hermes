# Signal Prospector (Hermes profile distribution)

Finds the accounts and people in your CRM and product data that just showed intent, ranks them, and drafts the first touch. Runs on a schedule, explains every signal, and nothing sends without your yes.

Install: `hermes profile install ./dists/signal-prospector --alias -y` (from the repo root) then `grok-signal-prospector chat`.

Skills: 5  Routines: 1  Integrations: Salesforce, HubSpot, Attio, Mixpanel, X, Hunter, Clay, Gmail, Slack
